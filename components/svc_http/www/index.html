Factory Reset

Erases all saved settings (WiFi credentials, location, prayer configuration) and restarts the device in setup mode. This cannot be undone.

Firmware Update

Upload a .bin firmware image. The device flashes the inactive OTA slot, reboots into it, and validates itself on the next boot. If the new image fails to reach the handoff stage, the bootloader reverts to the current firmware automatically.
